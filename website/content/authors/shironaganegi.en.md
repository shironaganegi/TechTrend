## Shironaganegi

I operate and edit TechTrend Watch. As an independent developer interested in machine learning,
LLMs, and developer tooling, I follow trends in AI and dev tools while keeping a hands-on,
practitioner's perspective.

### Areas of Focus
- Machine learning / Large Language Models (LLM), RAG, AI agents
- Individual development and automation, mostly in Python
- Comparing and testing developer-facing tools

### Editorial Policy
Articles start from primary sources such as GitHub and Hacker News, and are reconstructed from
"a working developer's point of view" — adding **comparisons, real-world pitfalls, and FAQs**.
The goal is not a mere summary but to help readers know what to do next.
Information reflects the state at publication; please verify in your own environment before adopting anything.
