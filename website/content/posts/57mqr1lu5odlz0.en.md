## 💡 Prosed's Core Features and Technical Approach

Prosed is not merely a simple wrapper system combining a transcription tool with ChatGPT. To elevate scattered content into a "book" with a coherent logical structure, it employs several sophisticated technical approaches:

### 1. Contextual Integration via Multimodal Integration
It seamlessly integrates audio data (MP3, WAV, etc.) with web-based text data (URLs from Substack, Medium, personal blogs, etc.). By combining state-of-the-art ASR (Automated Speech Recognition) technology with natural language processing (NLP) models, the system is designed to accurately extract the creator's "conceptual context" across different data formats.

### 2. Automated Chapter Structuring (Logical Outline Construction)
It analyzes relationships between topics across scattered episodes and reorganizes them into a sequence that is easiest for readers to understand. This is achieved by having Large Language Models (LLMs) scan the entire context window to autonomously design information hierarchy and logical structures (chapters, sections, and subsections).

### 3. Consistent Creator Style (Voice & Tone Profiling)
It eliminates the dry, robotic, explanatory tone typical of AI-generated text. By "profiling" the creator's unique phrasing, preferred rhetoric, technical jargon context, and humor from the input audio or past texts, it reflects these nuances in the output of the target language (including Japanese). This makes it possible to generate a highly natural manuscript that feels as though the creator wrote it themselves.

## ⚠️ Practical Caveats and the "Human-Centered" Editing Process

While Prosed is an exceptionally powerful tool, creating a "truly valuable book" that stands up to commercial publishing or paid distribution requires understanding the limitations of AI and properly integrating human input.

* **Ensuring Input Audio Quality (S/N Ratio)**
  When generating manuscripts from audio data like podcasts, microphone quality and ambient noise (Signal-to-Noise ratio) directly impact transcription accuracy. Providing a clean, clear audio source is essential to making the subsequent editing process infinitely easier.
* **Fact-Checking AI Hallucinations**
  AI is highly adept at weaving smooth transitions, but it can occasionally present non-existent data or factual errors with plausible authority. Especially when writing technical guides or books in finance, rigorous human fact-checking is indispensable.
* **Maximizing Value Through "Human-in-the-Loop"**
  An AI-generated manuscript typically reaches about "80% completion" in terms of structure and coherence. However, what truly captivates readers is the remaining 20%—the raw personal failures and unique interpretations of the writer. The most effective approach is a collaboration where AI builds the skeleton, and the human breathes life into it with their personal story.
