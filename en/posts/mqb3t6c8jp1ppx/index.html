The “Sanctuary” of AI Development Exposed? Behold the 30,000-Line Grimoire Powering Cursor and Devin#

“Why does that AI tool understand me so well?”

Every engineer has experienced that mysterious sensation at least once. Now, the true nature of that feeling is about to be brought into the light. As the Editor-in-Chief of Shiro-Negi Tech, I have discovered a collection of wisdom so “raw” that I hesitated for a moment before hitting the publish button.

I am talking about a GitHub repository that has been quietly but explosively gaining attention: “system-prompts-and-models-of-ai-tools”.

Cursor, Claude Code, Devin, v0… the “internal blueprints” (system prompts) of the latest AI agents we use every day, which often feel like magic, have been aggregated into a volume exceeding 30,000 lines. This is not just a collection of code snippets. It is no exaggeration to call it a modern-day grimoire for transforming AI from a mere “tool” into a true “partner.”

💡 What Makes This Project So “Insane”?#

Peeking into this repository is like sneaking into a world-class chef’s kitchen to steal the secret recipe for their signature sauce.

  • Hacking the Thought Process of Top Players: You can see exactly what “roles” and “thought sequences” tools with world-class UX, like Cursor and v0, assign to Large Language Models (LLMs). Every single move they make is documented.
  • The Mastery of Instruction in 30,000 Lines: How to make ambiguous instructions concrete and how to handle unexpected errors. This is a practical textbook on prompt engineering laid bare in a real-world format.
  • The Pulse of the Latest Agents: Prompts for tools that just became trending yesterday or today, such as Trae and Manus, are archived immediately. This might be the only vantage point from which to keep pace with the sheer speed of AI evolution.
  • Understanding the Vulnerability of “Defense”: This is also a lesson in security. It shows how hard startups work to hide their prompts—and how easily they can be exposed. This is the “reality” that every developer should know.

🚀 Reading It Is Learning the “Etiquette” of AI Interaction#

The usage is simple. Just dig through the directories and open the file for the tool you’re interested in. For example, if you open the “Claude Code” entry, you can directly decipher his “internal reflection process”—what he asks himself before he even touches the terminal.

# Conceptual image of internal instructions
- You are not just a code generator.
- Anticipate the user's intent and always "think one step ahead."
- Before making destructive changes, always kneel before the master (the human) and ask for confirmation...

You will likely be struck by the realization that this accumulation of meticulous, almost human-like instructions is the true source of that perceived “intelligence.”

🛠 How This Will Change Your “Development”#

  1. For AI “Personality Formation”: By referencing the “ultimate roleplay” written by pros, your own app’s AI will become significantly smarter tomorrow.
  2. Knowing the “Correct Answer” for Prompts: Moving from implicit knowledge (“it works if I say this”) to the certainty of knowing why it works.
  3. Dissecting Design Philosophies: Compare tools like Cursor and Windsurf, which are similar yet different. Seeing how their “philosophies” diverge at the prompt level is the ultimate intellectual exercise.

⚖️ Editor’s Perspective: The Brighter the Light, the Deeper the Shadow#

Of course, Shiro-Negi Tech wouldn’t be true to its name if we only offered uncritical praise.

  • The Upside: Its value as a learning resource is arguably the “best in the universe.” You won’t find better educational material unless you have access to private documents in Silicon Valley.
  • The Risk: We must not forget that these are not “official documents” intentionally released by the developers. They are, so to speak, “forbidden knowledge” obtained through analysis.

However, that is exactly why they are valuable. For an AI developer, knowing that your own prompts are “destined to be exposed like this” should serve as a powerful catalyst for your defensive instincts.

Summary: Holding the Anatomy of the “Magic Wand” in Your Hands#

Let’s be honest: over the next year, an unbridgeable gap will open between engineers who know this and engineers who simply watch from the sidelines thinking, “Wow, AI is amazing.”

Understanding the “backside” isn’t about dispelling the magic. It’s about learning how the magic is woven so that you can use it yourself. There is no telling how long this repository will remain online. Before it disappears, burn it into your memory and craft your own “ultimate prompt.”

Are you ready to satisfy your intellectual curiosity? All the answers are waiting inside this repository. 🚀🔥
