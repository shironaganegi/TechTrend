## public-apis: The "Knowledge Infrastructure" for Engineers

`public-apis` is more than just a collection of links. It is a massive index that categorizes free APIs from around the world based on the criteria that often drive developers crazy: authentication requirements, security (HTTPS), and browser restrictions (CORS).

💡 **Editor's "Hot Takes":**
- **Overwhelming "Granularity"**: It covers every genre imaginable, from animal images to financial data and even anime quotes.
- **Avoiding Implementation "Traps"**: Prevent the tragedy of "I tried to call it but got stuck on CORS" before it happens, thanks to a simple icon in the table.
- **Commitment to Freshness**: Because the community constantly monitors it, it doesn't devolve into a "dead list" of broken links.

## Start the Treasure Hunt: Tips for Finding APIs Fast

The usage is so simple it barely needs explanation:

1. Head to the [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/public-apis/public-apis) and take a deep breath.
2. Select a category from the Table of Contents based on your intuition.
3. Check "Auth," "HTTPS," and "CORS" on the right side of the list to ensure they match your environment.

For example, if you want to build a "Movie Recommendation App" over the weekend, just peek into the "Movies" category. There, you'll find several doors leading to an ocean of cinematic information.
