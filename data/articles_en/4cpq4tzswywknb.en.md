## 1. Three Core Metrics Defined by "Engineering Context"

- **Measured Network Throughput**: This is not just a "Wi-Fi available" flag. It displays a proprietary score representing effective speed and latency capable of withstanding `npm install`, pulling `Docker images`, or large-scale deployment tasks.
- **Power Density (Availability of Power Supply)**: In addition to the percentage of seats with outlets, it visualizes the "ease of securing power" during peak hours.
- **Environmental Sound Spectrum Analysis**: Not just a simple noise level (dB), but a segmentation based on use cases—whether the noise is white noise suitable for focus or quiet enough for web conferencing.

Notably, the platform uses algorithms to calculate "tolerance for long stays"—a factor that previously relied on subjective intuition—based on user stay logs and feedback. This design preemptively prevents "psychological conflict" between the user and the establishment.
