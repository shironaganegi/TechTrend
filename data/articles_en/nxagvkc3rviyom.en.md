## 🚀 4 Steps to Reclaim Your Freedom

No special skills are required for setup. You simply install the magic as a Google Slides add-on.

1. **Install**: Search for "Plus AI" on the [Google Workspace Marketplace](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/). It takes only a few clicks.
2. **Call the Agent**: Launch Plus AI from the Extensions menu. The sidebar that appears on the right is your new workspace.
3. **Communicate Intent**: Select "New Presentation" and freely write what you want to create. Bullet points or rough sentences are perfectly fine.
4. **Co-creation and Completion**: Review the outline proposed by the AI, and if you're satisfied, click "Generate." A few minutes later, you'll have a professional set of slides ready.
