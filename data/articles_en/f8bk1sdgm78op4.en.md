## What is freemocap? Obtaining the Magic Wand

**freemocap** is a platform born to realize "research-grade motion capture for anyone, anywhere, for free."

Until now, digitizing high-precision movement required specialized infrared cameras or expensive sensors. However, freemocap only requires the webcams or smartphone videos you already use every day. An "invisible choreographer" powered by AI meticulously maps out a skeletal structure from the footage down to the millimeter.

## Now, Let’s Cast the Spell

If you have even a bit of engineering blood in you, you'll find the simplicity of the setup incredibly satisfying. As long as you have a Python environment, your PC will evolve into a MoCap studio in minutes.

### 1. Installation
Prepare a Python 3.10–3.12 environment and simply run this single line:

```bash
pip install freemocap
```

### 2. Launching the GUI
Once ready, just shout this command into your terminal:

```bash
freemocap
```

With just that, a sophisticated interface appears before your eyes. Drop in a video file or connect a camera. Instantly, the "shadow" on the screen begins to move as if it has inherited your soul. This ease of use is so profound it moves past "impressive" and borders on "scary."

Of course, this isn't a magic wand that solves everything instantly.
